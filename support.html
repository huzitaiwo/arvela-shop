FAQs

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

Do I need to pay when I place an order?

No. When you place an order we receive it and email you to confirm everything, including delivery and payment details. You aren't charged at the moment you order.

How long will delivery take?

We confirm delivery timing by email once your order is confirmed, so you know what to expect before anything is arranged.

How do I check on my order?

Sign in and open My account. Your order history shows each order and its current status.

Is assembly difficult?

Our pieces ship flat-packed and are designed to be easy to assemble. Take a look at the assembly guide below for general tips.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Get in touch as soon as possible using the contact form, with your order number, and we'll do our best to help.

What if something arrives damaged?

Contact us with your order number and a photo of the damage, and we'll work with you to put it right.

How should I care for my furniture?

Wipe surfaces with a soft, slightly damp cloth and dry them straight away. Keep wood away from direct heat and strong sunlight, and use coasters under hot or wet items.

Shipping & Returns

Shipping

We ship flat-packed to keep delivery efficient and our packaging recyclable. After you place an order, we review it and email you to confirm delivery timing, shipping costs and payment details.

Returns

If something isn't right, contact us as soon as you receive your order. Include your order number and, if relevant, photos, and we'll work with you to put it right.

Assembly Guides

General tips that apply to every Arvela piece.

  1. Unpack and checkLay out every part and check it against the parts list before you start.
  2. Use a soft surfaceBuild on a rug or the packaging so the finish stays unmarked.
  3. Hand-tighten firstFit every screw loosely, then tighten them all fully at the end so parts align.
  4. Ask for a second pair of handsLarger pieces such as shelves and desks are easier and safer with two people.
  5. Tighten again after a few weeksFixings settle with use, so a quick re-tighten keeps everything solid.
