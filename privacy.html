This policy explains what personal information Arvela (“we”, “us”) collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. We've kept it short and plain on purpose.

Information we collect

We only collect what we need to run the store:

  • Account details: your name and email address when you create an account. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider, and we can't see them.
  • Order details: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, the items you order and any note you add.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you subscribe.
  • Messages: your name, email and the message you send through our contact form.
  • Cart: the items you add to your cart.

Our service providers also process technical information, such as your IP address and device details, to keep the service secure and working.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders, and to email you about them.
  • To create and run your account and show your order history.
  • To reply to your messages and questions.
  • To send our newsletter, only if you subscribed.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent abuse.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Google (Firebase): our provider for sign-in and for the database that stores accounts, orders and messages on our behalf.
  • Google Fonts: which delivers our typeface. Your browser contacts Google when a page loads, so Google receives your IP address.
  • Delivery partners: any carriers we use receive what they need to deliver your order.
  • Authorities: where the law requires it.

Your browser storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember what's in your cart, and our authentication provider uses browser storage to keep you signed in. Both are needed for the site to work. We don't currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we'll update this policy.

How long we keep it

  • Order records: for as long as we need them to run the business and meet legal and accounting obligations.
  • Your account: until you ask us to delete it.
  • Newsletter email: until you ask us to remove it.
  • Messages: for as long as needed to handle them.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to how we use it, or receive a copy. To make a request, or to unsubscribe from our newsletter, contact us through the contact form and we'll respond.

Children

Our website is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact form on our Support page.
